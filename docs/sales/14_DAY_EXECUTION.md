# 14-Day Sales Sprint (B2C, RU/CIS)

## Goal
Get first paid subscriptions for Enroll in 14 days via manual sales in Telegram/Instagram.

## Offer
- 7-day trial + onboarding call (20-30 min)
- Subscription after trial: 1490-1990 RUB/month
- First 20 users: price lock for 3 months

## Promise
- Clear deadlines, tasks, and documents in one place
- Structured admissions workflow for US applications

## Non-promise
- No guarantee of admission
- No automatic submission instead of user

## Day-by-day
### Day 1-2
- Prepare landing and pilot page
- Record 60-90 sec demo
- Prepare scripts and FAQ
- Build CRM table

### Day 3-5
- 15-20 outbound messages/day
- 5-8 demos total
- 5+ trial activations

### Day 6-10
- Daily follow-ups
- Mid-trial value recap on day 5
- First paid conversions

### Day 11-14
- Close remaining trial users
- Collect objections
- Adjust price and positioning

## KPI
- 80-120 contacts in 10 days
- 10+ active trial users
- 3+ paid users
- Trial-to-paid >= 20%

## Daily metrics
- outbound
- replies
- demos booked
- demos completed
- trial started
- paid
- top objections
